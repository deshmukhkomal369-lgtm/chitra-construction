About Chitra Construction

Chitra Construction was established in 2022 with a vision to deliver quality-driven and trustworthy construction services.
The company was founded by Mr. Akash Suryakant Jagtap (B.E. Civil), a passionate entrepreneur committed to creating meaningful and lasting infrastructure.

Chitra Construction specializes in residential, commercial, government, and turnkey projects. Our mission is to deliver high-quality construction with transparency, timely completion, and customer satisfaction.

With experienced engineers, modern equipment, and a skilled workforce, we transform ideas into durable and beautiful structures that stand the test of time.

The name "Chitra Construction" holds deep emotional significance. It is a heartfelt tribute to his late mother,
Mrs. Chitra Jagtap, whose values, strength and inspiration continue to guide the foundation of the company.
